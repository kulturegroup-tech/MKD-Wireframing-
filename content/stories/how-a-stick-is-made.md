Every stick passes through four stages before the carton closes. None of them can be rushed.

## 1. Roll

Women artisans roll the masala onto thin bamboo by hand, stick after stick, keeping the coat even so that it burns evenly. More than a thousand of them work with us across our units.

![Freshly rolled sticks drying on wooden trays in a rack room](brand/make-rest.webp)

## 2. Rest

The rolled sticks are laid out on racks and dried batch by batch. A stick that is packed too early burns badly, so drying takes as long as it takes.

## 3. Fragrance

Each batch is perfumed in Kanpur to the house formula, the way it has been since 1995. The oils are weighed and measured for every batch, so a Tiranga Chandan smells the same wherever it is sold.

![A perfumer pouring fragrance oil over a tray of incense sticks](brand/make-fragrance.webp)

## 4. Pack

Sticks are checked, counted and packed in our own units, then sent to distributors in 500+ cities.

![Women counting incense sticks into small cartons at a packing table](brand/make-pack.webp)

## What we hold ourselves to

- **Hand-rolled.** Every stick starts in human hands.
- **Natural ingredients.** Fragrance-led formulas without chemical shortcuts.
- **Made in-house.** Formula to carton, under one roof.
- **Built for partners.** Distribution, export and private label from the same plants.
