Our story did not begin with fragrance. It began with cartons.

## 1965: a print and box unit

In Kanpur, the family ran a print and box unit. Printing and folding cartons for other people's products taught us how things are made, packed and moved across north India, one printed carton at a time. It also taught us what a shopkeeper needs from a pack: that it arrives on time, survives the journey and sells itself from the shelf.

## 1995: Tiranga is born

Thirty years of packaging know-how became an incense house. The first Tiranga agarbatti left Kanpur in 1995, carrying the promise that became its jingle: *Desh ki Khushboo*, the fragrance of the country. Familiar fragrances, honest prices, and a stick that burns evenly from tip to end.

## Today: ten plants and 500+ cities

Tiranga is now the everyday agarbatti of north India, loved by rural and middle-income homes. It was joined by Sacred, *Dedicated to God*, a premium range for the complete puja and the home of the Diwali Poojan Kit. Ten plants, six of them in Kanpur, make thirteen million sticks a day, with more than a thousand women artisans and a distribution network across 500+ cities.

## Next: beyond India

Sacred Selections takes the house abroad for the first time: small-batch, charcoal-free incense, hand-packed in Kanpur in keepsake boxes for homes around the world.

Six decades on, it is still one family business, and still made in Kanpur.
