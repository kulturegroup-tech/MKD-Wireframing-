## Where we deliver

We deliver to every serviceable pincode in India. We don't ship outside India yet. For orders from abroad, or for bulk and trade supply, [write to us](contact.html).

## Delivery charges

- **Free delivery** on orders of ₹499 and above.
- Orders below ₹499 have a delivery charge of ₹49, shown at checkout before you pay.
- Cash on delivery has an extra charge of ₹40, also shown at checkout.

## How long it takes

We pack and dispatch from our plant in Kanpur within 1 to 2 working days. Most orders then reach you in 2 to 5 working days, depending on your location and the courier. Remote areas can take a little longer. The time shown at checkout is an estimate, not a guarantee.

## Tracking your order

When your order ships, we send you the courier name and tracking number by SMS, WhatsApp or email. You can follow it on the courier's website or on [Shiprocket tracking](https://www.shiprocket.in/shipment-tracking/).

## If something goes wrong

If your parcel is late, arrives damaged or looks opened, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo. See [Returns and refunds](refunds.html) for what happens next.
