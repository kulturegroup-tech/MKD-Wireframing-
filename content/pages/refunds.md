Because our products are used in worship and are consumable, we can't take back items that have been opened or used, and we don't accept returns for a change of mind. We will replace or refund your order in these cases:

1. **Damaged or defective products.** The pack arrives broken, crushed or spoilt.
2. **The wrong product.** You received something other than what you ordered.
3. **Missing items.** Something from your order is not in the parcel.

## How to ask for a replacement or refund

Contact us **within 24 hours of delivery** with your order number and photos of the parcel and the products. Please keep the packaging until we have sorted it out. We review every request and reply within 2 working days.

If your request is approved, we either send a replacement at no cost or refund you, as you prefer.

## How refunds are paid

- **Paid online:** the money goes back to the same UPI account, card or bank account you paid from, within 5 to 7 working days of approval.
- **Cash on delivery:** we refund to your bank account or UPI ID within 5 to 7 working days of approval.

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order free of charge until it is dispatched. Call or WhatsApp us with your order number. Once it has shipped, it can't be cancelled.
