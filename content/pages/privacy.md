This policy explains how Maa Kamakhya Darbar Fragrances (India) Pvt. Ltd. handles your personal data when you use this website.

## What we collect

- **When you order:** your name, mobile number, email (if you give it) and delivery address, and what you ordered.
- **When you pay online:** PhonePe processes your payment. We receive a payment reference and status, never your card, UPI PIN or bank details.
- **When you browse:** your cart is kept in your own browser. We don't use advertising cookies.

## How we use it

- To deliver your order and contact you about it.
- To handle returns, refunds and questions.
- To meet our legal and tax obligations.

We don't sell your data. We share it only with the people who help us complete your order: the courier and Shiprocket for delivery, PhonePe for payments, and our email provider for order messages.

## How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, currently up to 8 years. You can ask us to delete anything we don't need to keep by law.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent for messages, by writing to [contact@mkdtiranga.com](mailto:contact@mkdtiranga.com). We reply within 30 days.

## Security

Orders are stored with access limited to our team, and payments run on PhonePe's secure pages. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to protect your information.
