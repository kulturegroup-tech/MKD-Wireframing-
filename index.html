How a stick is made

Every stick passes through four stages and many pairs of hands before the carton closes.

  1. 1

    Roll

    Women artisans roll the masala onto bamboo by hand. Over a thousand of them work with us across our units.

  2. 2

    Rest

    Sticks dry on racks batch by batch, until each one burns evenly from tip to end.

  3. 3

    Fragrance

    Each batch is perfumed in Kanpur to the house formula, the way it has been since 1995.

  4. 4

    Pack

    Sticks are checked, counted and packed in our own units, then sent to distributors in 500+ cities.
